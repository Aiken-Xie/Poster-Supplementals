DRBX · APS DPP poster supplement

Field-aligned turbulence modeling for stellarator edge physics

DRBX is a JAX-based drift-reduced Braginskii code for edge and scrape-off-layer turbulence in tokamaks and stellarators.

Why it matters

Strong parallel–perpendicular anisotropy motivates field-line mapping. DRBX combines an η-based FCI construction with wall-fitted, axis-regular coordinates to make that approach practical in HSX-like stellarator geometry.

DRBX is developed for research in edge and scrape-off-layer turbulence. Links to the poster PDF, source repository, citations, and acknowledgments will be added with the final media.
